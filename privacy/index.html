Privacy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Information you provide

If you submit a business efficiency assessment form, the site collects the details shown in that form, such as your name, company, work email and the information you choose to include in your message. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the service you requested.

Services used by this website

Form submissions are delivered using FormSubmit. The optional chat widget is provided by Chatbase; information entered into that widget is handled by that service. Links to LinkedIn and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Retention and sharing

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable obligations. It is not sold. It may be handled by the service providers needed to operate the website and deliver your enquiry.

Your questions

To ask about information submitted through this website, or to request a correction or deletion where applicable, email contact@vasthorizons.ie.
