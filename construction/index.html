Where time gets lost

Project work creates information friction.

The opportunity is often not one dramatic automation. It is a set of smaller improvements that help the right information reach the right person with less chasing and rework.

01

Tenders & quotations

Structure documents, surface exclusions, support comparisons and prepare review notes.

02

RFIs & variations

Capture requests, track responses, connect supporting information and flag outstanding actions.

03

Project knowledge

Make approved information easier to find across email, meeting notes and document stores.

04

Document processing

Extract known fields, classify incoming files and route items for the right human review.

05

Meeting actions

Turn notes into owned actions, reminders and clear status updates without duplicate entry.

06

Connected systems

Reduce manual handoffs between project tools, CRM, finance and document management.

These are assessment opportunities, not completed-project claims. Suitability depends on the workflow, data quality, contractual context and required controls.
