The assessment

Start with one recurring problem.

Together, we look at what happens, where time or value is lost and which improvements are worth pursuing.

  1. 01

    Understand the work

    Follow the job from enquiry and quote through scheduling, job records, invoicing and follow-up.

  2. 02

    Find what slows it down

    Look for waiting, chasing, repeated entry and missing details, especially where work stalls or depends on one person being available.

  3. 03

    Choose what is worth improving

    Prioritise the strongest opportunities, how significant they appear and which improvements are worth pursuing.

See examples of work we can assess

Enquiries

Capture the caller's need, location and preferred response so follow-up reaches the right person.

Quotes

Prepare consistent quotes, track what was sent and prompt the next appropriate follow-up.

Scheduling

Keep bookings, changes, arrival information and confirmations clearer for the team and customer.

Job records

Turn site notes, photos and key details into a structured record that is easy to find later.

Invoices & paperwork

Reduce repeated entry, organise supporting documents and make outstanding invoices and paperwork easier to track.

Routine communications

Support reminders, confirmations and routine updates while people handle exceptions and decide what happens next.

These are examples to assess, not completed-client claims. Suitability depends on your business, customers, existing tools and the judgement required.

Where an improvement is worth pursuing, Vast Horizons designs, builds and implements the solution: a clearer process, connected tools, automation or AI where it adds real value.
